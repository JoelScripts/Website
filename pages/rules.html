Community Rules

Rules for the community

This page has been rebuilt to feel simpler, cleaner, and easier to read. The Discord server rules and Twitch chat rules are separated below in a calm, uncluttered layout.

Discord Community
12 rules

Discord server rules

These rules help keep the server respectful, organised, and enjoyable for everyone in the community.

  1. 🤝
    Rule 01

    Be respectful to everyone

    No hate speech, harassment, bullying, discrimination, or personal attacks. Take personal issues to DMs.

  2. 📵
    Rule 02

    No spamming

    Avoid excessive messages, emoji spam, reaction spam, and repeated bot commands. Keep chat readable.

  3. 🛡️
    Rule 03

    Keep content safe for work

    No NSFW images, videos, gore, or suggestive content anywhere in the server.

  4. 🚫
    Rule 04

    No illegal activity

    No piracy, hacking, scams, or anything that breaks Discord rules or platform guidelines.

  5. 📢
    Rule 05

    No unsolicited self-promotion

    Do not advertise your Twitch, YouTube, Discord, socials, or other platforms without permission.

  6. 🎯
    Rule 06

    Respect stream boundaries

    No spoilers, disruptive behaviour, or off-topic spam during live events. Let Joel and the mods handle moderation.

  7. 🗂️
    Rule 07

    Use the right channels

    Keep flight sim talk, emote ideas, tech help, and other topics in the correct channels.

  8. 🎭
    Rule 08

    No impersonation

    Do not pretend to be Joel, a moderator, or another community member.

  9. 🔗
    Rule 09

    Keep links clean

    Only share safe, relevant links. No malicious URLs, clickbait, or inappropriate links.

  10. 🔒
    Rule 10

    Protect privacy

    No doxxing and no sharing personal information about yourself or anyone else without consent.

  11. ⏳
    Rule 11

    Wait before submitting unban requests

    If you are banned from Twitch chat, wait at least 7 days before submitting an appeal or unban request.

  12. ⚖️
    Rule 12

    No alt accounts or ban evasion

    Do not use alternate accounts to bypass mutes, timeouts, bans, or any other moderation action.

Twitch Community
11 rules

Twitch chat rules

These rules keep stream chat clear, friendly, and welcoming for both regular viewers and new visitors.

  1. 💬
    Rule 01

    No hate speech, harassment, bullying, or drama

    Respect everyone in chat and take personal issues to DMs instead of bringing them into stream chat.

  2. 📵
    Rule 02

    Keep messages, emojis, and bot commands reasonable

    No spam walls, repeated copy-paste, or flooding chat in a way that disrupts the stream.

  3. 🛡️
    Rule 03

    No NSFW, gore, or suggestive content

    Keep chat safe for work and appropriate for the whole community.

  4. 🚫
    Rule 04

    No piracy, hacking, or anything against platform guidelines

    Do not encourage or discuss illegal activity that breaks Twitch, Discord, or community rules.

  5. 📢
    Rule 05

    Do not advertise your channels or servers

    No self-promo for your Twitch, YouTube, Discord, socials, or communities unless invited.

  6. 🎯
    Rule 06

    No spoilers, off-topic spam, or disrupting live events

    Help keep the stream flowing by staying on-topic and not derailing big moments.

  7. 🎭
    Rule 07

    Do not pretend to be Joel, mods, or other users

    Impersonation causes confusion and will be treated seriously.

  8. 🔗
    Rule 08

    No clickbait, NSFW, or malicious URLs

    Only share links that are safe, relevant, and appropriate for stream chat.

  9. 🔒
    Rule 09

    No doxxing or sharing personal info

    Never post private or identifying information about yourself or anyone else.

  10. ⏳
    Rule 10

    Wait 7 days before submitting an appeal

    If you are banned, do not rush an unban request immediately. Appeals sent too early may be denied.

  11. ⚖️
    Rule 11

    Bypassing moderation leads to more penalties

    Using alt accounts or trying to evade moderation actions will lead to further punishment.

Breaking these rules may lead to message removal, timeouts, kicks, or bans depending on the situation and severity. The goal is always to keep the community welcoming, safe, and enjoyable for everyone.
If something needs moderation attention, contact the mod team instead of escalating it publicly in chat.
